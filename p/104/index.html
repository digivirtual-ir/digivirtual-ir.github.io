Description

This UK Residence Documentation PSD template reproduces the Home Office residence documentation set — the front residence card and the 6-page Home Office booklet — as fully editable, layered Photoshop files at A4 size (2480×3425 pixels, 300 DPI). Every field is a separate text layer, so any value can be changed in Adobe Photoshop or the free browser editor Photopea without damaging the original design, and the photo layer is replaced with a couple of clicks.

Authentic details included in this template

  1. Two complete PSD files: the front residence card template and the scan-style Home Office booklet version with its full inside pages
  2. Card header: RESIDENCE DOCUMENTATION with the UKF series and the document number field (0000000 placeholder)
  3. Holder name block: JOHN, CITIZEN WILLIAMS with surname and given-name lines plus a dedicated Document Renewal Date field
  4. Place and date of issue: the LIVERPOOL and 00 SEP/SEPT 00 placeholders ready for any city and date
  5. Type Of Document line set to Certifying Permanent Residence, with a remarks block showing the 00 JAN/JAN 00, M and BGR placeholders
  6. Two-line machine-readable zone printed in authentic OCR B typeface, opening with the surname segment VRGBRWILLIAMS and carrying document-number, BGR and M placeholders on the second line
  7. Home Office booklet pages: UNITED KINGDOM OF GREAT BRITAIN AND NORTHERN IRELAND letterhead, the residence documentation for a national of an EEA State under Directive 0000/00/EC, E0000000 page numbering and the immigration officer notice
  8. Three matching fonts (OCR B 10 BT, Courier New and Baskerville Old Face) inside the package, print-ready 300 DPI layout, works in Adobe Photoshop and Photopea on Windows, macOS and mobile
