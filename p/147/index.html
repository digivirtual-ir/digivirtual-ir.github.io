Description

This Taiwan university invoice is a faithful A4 tuition payment slip of the kind a student receives when a Taiwanese university issues the bill for a semester. The issuing institution is National Taiwan Ocean University, whose campus stands on Bei Ning Road in the Zhongzheng district of Keelung, and the document reproduces the structure that Taiwanese collection slips normally follow: an institutional header with contact details, a student identification block, a long itemised fee table, a settlement panel carrying the transfer account, a panel of accepted payment channels with their deadlines, and a numbered notice block. The telephone number given for the finance office is 2462-2192 with extensions 1145 and 2106, and the notice explains that the cashier counter is staffed on weekdays between 10:30 and 12:30, with different hours announced during enrolment.

The body of the slip is a seventeen line fee table in the conventional university format, with one column for tuition and miscellaneous charges and a second column for insurance, accommodation and service fees, each followed by a remarks column. Tuition is set at 16960 and the miscellaneous fee at 10850, while student accident insurance comes to 214 and the computer and network communication fee to 300. The remaining lines, including the credit and course fee, the tuition base, the graduate credit fee, the education programme fee, the dormitory fee and its deposit, the international student health insurance and both waived-fee lines, are printed as zero, so a student can see at a glance exactly which items apply to their programme and which do not. A waiver category field is left blank for manual completion.

The settlement details sit below the fee table. Payments are collected into transfer account 1108301153114020, and the slip names the bank that accepts payment at any of its branches, through its own cash machines and by credit card, with convenience stores also taking the payment for a small additional handling fee. Two separate deadline lines are printed, one for newly enrolled students and a later one for returning students, and both carry the corresponding convenience store cut-off, with a clear statement that payment after the deadline is refused and must instead be settled at the finance office. A five item notice closes the document, covering the delay fee that applies to late payers, the way fee reductions and subsidies must be deducted before a student loan is taken, the amounts that may be borrowed for housing and books, the internal extension for filing loan paperwork before the registration date, and the portal eschool.firstbank.com.tw where a student can reprint a lost receipt.
