Description

This Nigeria driver license template in PS/PSD format reproduces the «National Drivers Licence» card of the Federal Republic of Nigeria with both front and back sides on a 1060×1480 px canvas. All fields are real text objects editable in Photoshop, and the archive ships with all three required fonts (arial regular, Jey, ARLRDBD) so the design matches from the first open.

Authentic fields contained in this template

  1. Card header: «FEDERAL REPUBLIC OF NIGERIA / NATIONAL DRIVERS LICENCE» with the Nigerian flag and the issuing state «EDO STATE»
  2. Licence strip: the number «L/NO EKP00000AA» with usage «PRIVATE» and class «CLASS D»
  3. Holder block: the name «JOHN CITIZEN» and the address «EDO CEMENT PLANT, OKPELLA, EDO» with two photos and the date of birth «D of B 00-00-0000»
  4. Physical data: «SEX M», «BG O» (blood group), «HT 0.00M» (height), «F/MARKS N» and «GL N» (glasses)
  5. Dates: issue «ISS 00-00-0000», expiry «EXP 00-00-0000», first issue «DATE OF 1st ISS» and first-issue state «1st ISS ST EDO»
  6. Counters and endorsements: «REP 0» (replacements), «REN 0» (renewals), «END P» (endorsement P-Passenger) and «N of K 0000000000» (next of kin)
  7. Signature zone: «HOLDER'S SIGNATURE» signed J.Citizen plus the «AUTHORISED SIGNATORY» field
  8. Back side: barcode, the 16-digit number «0000000000000000», the national coat of arms, the definition «CLASS OF LICENCE D: Motor vehicle, other than motor-cycle, taxi, stage carriage or omnibus but excluding an articulated vehicle or vehicle drawing a trailer» and the «INDEX» legend
