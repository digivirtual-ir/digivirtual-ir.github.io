  1. Card header: bilingual title 외국인등록증 (ALIEN REGISTRATION CARD) with the KOR code chip and the taeguk hologram reading KOREA IMMIGRATION SERVICE

  4. Country/region (국가/지역): UNITED STATES
  5. Status of sojourn (체류자격): 방문취업 (H-2) — the Working Visit programme
  6. Date of issue (발급일자): 2021.09.08, issued by 울산출입국·외국인사무소 (CHIFF. ULSAN IMMIGRATION OFFICE)
