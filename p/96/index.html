Description

The Latvia Residence Permit Card PSD Template is a fully layered, print-ready Photoshop file that reproduces both sides of the Latvian residence permit issued by the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs. The front carries the bilingual UZTURĒŠANĀS ATĻAUJA header with document number UA8759355, while the back holds the personal data block, chip artwork, barcode and the three-line machine-readable zone that starts with IRLVAUA8759355. Everything from the portrait photo to the guilloche rosette sits on its own named layer, so the card can be personalised in minutes and exported as crisp PNG or PDF.

What the template reproduces

  1. Header UZTURĒŠANĀS ATĻAUJA with document number UA8759355 in the top corner
  2. Surname and given names block — sample holder TRUMP DONALD JOHN
  3. Valid-until line showing 25.05.2024 beside the issuing office PMLP RIGAS 4.NODAĻA and issue date 24.05.2019
  4. Permit type PASTAVĪGA / PERMANENT printed under the Atļaujas veids label
  5. Back side with date of birth 14.06.1946, place USA, sex V/M and citizenship USA
  6. Personal code 140646-54109 above the barcode with the note about the right to work without restrictions
  7. Three-line MRZ beginning IRLVAUA8759355 for scanner-accurate typography
  8. Smart layers for portrait, ghost photo, signature 046752, chip, EU stars and the Latvian coat of arms
