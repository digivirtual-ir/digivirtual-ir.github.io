Paglalarawan

Ang Thailand driving license template na ito ay muling bumubuo ng „Private Motorcycle Driving Licence (Temporary)“ card ng Kaharian ng Thailand bilang layered PSD file na 2480×3425 px. Naka-imbak ang harap at likod na bahagi sa dalawang hiwalay na grupo, at ang bawat label, pangalan at petsa ay nakalagay sa tunay na Photoshop text layer, kaya mapapalitan mo ang lahat ng placeholder na detalye nang hindi nasira ang orihinal na disenyo ng kard.

Mga tunay na elemento na nasa file na ito

  1. Header ng harap: Thai flag at royal Garuda emblem kasama ang mga linya na „ประเทศไทย / Kingdom of Thailand“ at „ใบอนุญาตขับรถยานยนต์ส่วนบุคคล / Private Motorcycle Driving Licence (Temporary)“
  2. Dwì-linggwahe na mga field sa harap: „No. 00000000“, „วันออกบัตร / Issue Date 0 January 0000“, „วันสิ้นอายุ / Expiry Date“ at mga buwan sa Thai: มกราคม at เมษายน
  3. Name block: „ชื่อ / Name“ na may placeholder na pangalang JOHN CITIZEN WILLIAMS at photo frame na handang palitan
  4. Identity line: „เลขประจำตัวประชาชน / ID No. A0000000“ at „วันเกิด / Birth Date 0 April 0000“
  5. Ang asul na bandila sa ibaba: „นายทะเบียนจังหวัด ภูเก็ต“ — Provincial Registrar ng Phuket na may decorative circular pattern
  6. Likod na bahagi: license number „00 00 0000 0000“, itim na magnetic stripe, QR code at motorcycle icon
  7. Address block: „ที่อยู่ / Address“ na may teksto 000/00 Karon, Muang Phuket, Phuket at bersyon sa Thai: โคกโตนด ต.กะรน อ.เมือง จ.ภูเก็ต
  8. Signature field: ang pirma (กานดา สันติภัทรานนท์) sa itaas ng „นายทะเบียน / Authority“
