  2. Tax ID ng juridical person 0994002548971, invoice number IN22010339, house number 79/108, petsa ng pag-isyu at due date na parehong 31/01/22
  3. Bill-to block: Ginoong Wichai Witthayakitlerd (นายวิชัย วิทยาเกียรติเลิศ), 79/108 Village No. 17, D Condo Campus Resort, Chaeng Rak Road, Khlong Nueng, Khlong Luang, Pathum Thani 12120
  4. Anim na linya ng arrears para sa common-area fee (เงินเพิ่ม - ค่าใช้จ่ายส่วนกลาง) mula 11/2021 hanggang 04/2022, bawat isa 31.00 units sa 15.19 kada unit
  5. Balance section: common areas 9,030.00, tubig 80.00 at surcharge 91.14, na nagdadala sa arrears ng 9,201.14; kasalukuyang invoice ay nagdaragdag ng 91.14
  6. Kabuuang halaga na 9,292.28 baht na nakasulat sa salita sa Thai (เก้าพันสองร้อยเก้าสิบสองบาทยี่สิบแปดสตางค์)
